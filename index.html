What it does

Three things, done automatically.

Inbox on autopilot

Nova reads the whole thread, drafts a reply that sounds like you, and sends it once you approve. Routine confirmations it can clear on its own; anything sensitive it holds back and flags.

Self-healing calendar

When meetings collide, Nova reshuffles the movable ones and guards your focus blocks. It sends the reschedule notes itself, so most clashes are fixed before you even see them.

Notes to tasks

Paste a wall of meeting notes and Nova pulls out every action item, owner, and due date. The clean checklist syncs straight to Linear, Asana, or Notion.

How it works

Set up once. Nova runs in the background.

No workflow builder and no rules to maintain — connect your tools, set what needs your sign-off, and Nova learns the rest.

  1. 1

    Connect your tools

    Link your inbox, calendar, and task tracker in a couple of clicks. Nova reads recent history to pick up your tone and priorities.

  2. 2

    Set your guardrails

    Tell Nova what it can send on its own and what needs your approval. Adjust the boundaries any time from one screen.

  3. 3

    Review the daily digest

    Each morning, approve the drafts Nova prepared and see everything it already handled while you were away.
